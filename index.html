Welcome to Robert Zheng's Art Website

Robert Zheng is associated with the Draper Visual Arts Foundation, a Utah organization dedicated to supporting and promoting the visual arts. Drawing deep inspiration from the natural world, he frequently hikes and photographs the Utah mountains—experiences that directly inform his paintings. Beyond his artistic practice, Zheng serves as a Professor of Educational Psychology at the University of Utah, where his research focuses on cognition and computer-based education.
